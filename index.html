About Me

Hi, I'm a business and data analyst who loves turning messy data into clear answers that actually help people make decisions. I recently graduated with my Master of Science in Business Analytics from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, and I've spent the last 3+ years working at the intersection of finance, data operations, and process automation.

At Intellimation.ai, I worked on automation initiatives for tier-1 banking clients, helping bridge the gap between business needs and technical solutions. My days involved writing business requirements, collaborating with stakeholders and delivery teams, and building data-driven solutions that streamlined operations and reduced manual work.

I work mostly with SQL, R, and various BI tools to build solutions that are practical, measurable, and (hopefully) make someone's job a little easier. I'm drawn to projects where analytics can genuinely improve how things run—whether that's through automation, predictive modeling, or just good old-fashioned data cleanup.

When I'm not buried in data, you'll find me in the kitchen or at the gym, or trying (and often failing) at knitting. I also share my home with eight cats, who are excellent at debugging by sitting on my keyboard.
